Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know
before deciding.

How long does a print take?+
It depends on the square metres, resolution and surface type. As a guide, the machine prints roughly 1 m² per hour and setup takes about an hour, so a 20 m² mural usually needs 2–3 working days. We give you the exact timeline in the quote and, if needed, work in sections or outside your opening hours.
What guarantee do you offer?+
All indoor work includes a written 5-year guarantee, with the terms set out in the quote. If the print deteriorates prematurely under normal use, we fix it at no extra cost.
Can you create the design if I don't have one?+
Yes. We adapt your file for printing (format, resolution and fit to the wall) at no cost. If you need a new design from scratch, we quote it separately before starting. The free 3D render lets you approve the result before printing.
Why Octaprint and not adhesive vinyl?+
Adhesive vinyl can yellow and peel at the corners over time, and it does not bond well to rough surfaces. Octaprint prints directly onto the surface, with no adhesive and no seams, and the work carries a written 5-year guarantee. No renovation and no odours.
What surfaces does it work on?+
Almost all: plaster, emulsion paint, wood, stone, brick, clinker, metal, glass, ceramics and tiles, including rough render. The machine's sensors adjust the print head to wall irregularities. We run a test on the surface before each project.
What's the maximum print height?+
Minimum wall height: 1.7 m. Maximum print height: 4 metres (with the vertical axis extension). Width is unlimited. Technical margins: 6 cm left, 10 cm right and 25 cm top and bottom.
How long does the print last and can it be removed?+
The UV inks resist light, humidity and abrasion, and indoor work carries a written 5-year guarantee. To remove it: on glass, with a scraper; on other surfaces, with 2–3 coats of paint.
What file format do I need for the design?+
We accept AI, EPS, TIF, PNG, JPG and BMP. If the resolution is not enough, we help you adapt the file; if a new design is needed, we quote it separately.
Do you travel anywhere in Spain to print?+
We work mainly in Barcelona and Catalonia, and also take on projects elsewhere in Spain and in Andorra. Travel is free within 30 km of Barcelona; for longer distances the cost is shown in the quote. No hidden costs.
