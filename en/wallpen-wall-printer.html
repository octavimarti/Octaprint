What the wallPen E2 can't do

  • It doesn't reach right into the cornerLeave about 6 cm on the left, 10 cm on the right and 25 cm at the floor and ceiling.
  • No white without the optionGlass, dark or coloured walls need the fifth white channel, which doubles printing time.
  • Not for deep reliefIt prints on surfaces with up to about 10 mm of depth.

Frequently asked questions

Are businesses already making a living from it?

Yes, across Europe. For example, according to wallPen, the first wallPen E2 customer (Wall-Ink, Czech Republic) printed more than 2,500 m² in a year with two machines. Your first market, though, is always your own customers.

How much does the wallPen E2 cost?

The BASIC costs €44,000 + VAT and the ADVANCED €51,093 + VAT. The white printing option is €6,890 + VAT. These are the manufacturer's public prices; the quote itemises transport and options.

What's included?

The machine, an 800 ml ink starter kit, flushing liquid, toolbox, Android tablet, transport bags, wallPen OS 3.0 software, two days of factory training for two people, 12 months of support and updates and the manufacturer's warranty.

BASIC or ADVANCED?

The BASIC prints up to 2.4 m high, enough for most offices, shops and homes. The ADVANCED reaches 4 m and includes the 660 cm track and the Apex and Track Guard systems. If you'll work in lobbies, warehouses or museums, choose the ADVANCED.

Can it be financed?

Yes. We help you plan the investment as a monthly payment with finance providers. As a guide, wallPen publishes an example of about €773 a month over 60 months for the basic configuration, at a fixed financing cost of 5.4% (October 2026 prices). Each provider sets its own terms; the calculator shows how many jobs a month cover it.

How do I pay and when does it arrive?

Usually 50% on order confirmation and 50% before delivery. According to wallPen, delivery is usually about four weeks after the order; the exact date is agreed when ordering.

Do I need previous experience?

No. The official two-day training in Wissen is included, and we support you on your first projects.

How fast is it?

Between 1 and 16 m² per hour of pure printing time, depending on quality. A white layer doubles the time.

Which surfaces can it print on?

Plaster, paint, wallpaper, wood, brick, natural stone, concrete, metal, glass, plastic and most tiles, with up to about 10 mm of depth. Dark or transparent backgrounds need the white channel.

How much does the ink cost?

Between €1 and €5 per m², depending on the design; consumption is about 5 ml per m². A 200 ml bottle costs €88 + VAT in the wallPen shop.

What warranty and technical service does it have?

12 months on the machine and 48 months personal warranty on components for the first buyer. Support and updates are included for 12 months. We coordinate technical service and spare parts with the manufacturer.

What power supply does it need?

A standard socket, 110 to 240 V. It draws about 200 W while printing.

Can it be used outdoors?

Yes, but it lasts less: according to wallPen, about 2 years of UV resistance, longer with a clear protective coat.
