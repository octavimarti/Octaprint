Frequently asked questions

What does the wallPen E2 include?

According to wallPen, the package includes the machine, a 12- or 24-month ink flat rate depending on the package, the vertical axis for 1.7–2.4 m height and the app for Windows, macOS, iOS and Android. We detail the configuration in your quote.

What power supply does it need?

It runs on standard household voltage between 100 and 250 V.

How high does it print?

Minimum set-up height is 1.7 m and maximum 4 m, with unlimited width.
