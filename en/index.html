Frequently asked questions

How much does a mural cost?

It depends on the square metres, the surface and the design. Send us a photo and the size on WhatsApp and we will prepare a fixed quote, with a free preview render within 24 hours.

How long does it take?

As a guide, the machine prints about 1 m² per hour and set-up takes around one hour. A 20 m² mural usually needs 2–3 working days.

What guarantee do you offer?

A written 5-year guarantee for indoor work. The UV inks resist light, humidity and rubbing.

Can it be removed if I get tired of the design?

Yes. On most surfaces 2–3 coats of paint are enough. On glass, with a scraper.
