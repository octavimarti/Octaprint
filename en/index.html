All about wall mural printing

Direct wall printing for businesses and homes

Vertical printing adapts to any space where a wall can say something: a reception that presents the brand, a restaurant that wants guests to take photos, a children's room with its own world or a museum that needs room graphics without building panels. We print to the exact size of the wall, floor to ceiling if needed, and work by zones or after hours so your activity doesn't stop.

Wall murals for offices, hotels and shops

For companies, hotels, shops and interior design studios, the wall is part of the brand identity. We print logos, corporate values, headboards, lobbies and shop windows directly on the wall, with no vinyl and no seams. No renovation or closing needed: the UV ink cures instantly and is solvent-free. For architects and interior designers, we check the file, colour profile and dimensions before printing, and deliver the project with a fixed price and date.

Custom murals for homes

If you want a wall in your home to be unique, we print the design you choose: your own photograph, an illustration, a landscape or a custom artwork. It works in the living room, bedroom, children's room or any interior wall. The mural becomes part of the surface, with no lifting edges and a written 5-year guarantee. And if one day you want a change, just paint over it.

Direct UV printing vs decorative vinyl and wallpaper

Vinyl and wallpaper are stuck onto the wall; direct printing becomes part of the wall. That difference removes the usual problems: seams between panels, lifting edges, bubbles and glue residue when removed. Vinyl also needs a smooth, prepared wall, while the wallPen also prints on rough surfaces.

No seams, no bubbles and a 5-year guarantee

With no adhesive film, there is nothing to peel off with humidity or heat, no bubbles and no visible seams. The vegan, solvent-free UV ink dries instantly and resists light, humidity and rubbing. That is why we give a written 5-year guarantee for indoor work.

Printing on brick, concrete, wood, glass and metal

The vertical printer adapts to the surface you already have: plaster and paint, exposed brick, concrete, stone, wood, ceramic, metal and glass. Sensors adjust the print head to the wall's irregularities, and we test on the material before every project. Where vinyl struggles to stick, such as brick or rough concrete, direct printing is the answer.

Wall mural printing prices and free render

The price depends on the square metres, the surface type and the complexity of the design, so every quote is custom and fixed. Frequently asked questions

How much does a mural cost?

It depends on the square metres, the surface and the design. Send us a photo and the size on WhatsApp and we will prepare a fixed quote, with a free preview render within 24 hours. What the price depends on →

How long does it take?

As a guide, the machine prints about 1 m² per hour and set-up takes around one hour. A 20 m² mural usually needs 2–3 working days.

What guarantee do you offer?

A written 5-year guarantee for indoor work. The UV inks resist light, humidity and rubbing.

Can it be removed if I get tired of the design?

Yes. On most surfaces 2–3 coats of paint are enough. On glass, with a scraper.
