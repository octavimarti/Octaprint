Frequently asked questions

Can you print our company logo?

Yes. We accept AI, EPS, TIF, PNG, JPG and BMP and adapt the file for printing at no cost. New designs from scratch are quoted separately.

Do we need to close the office?

No. We work by zones or after hours so your activity continues. The ink dries instantly with UV LED curing.

How long does a print take?

As a guide, the machine prints about 1 m² per hour and set-up takes around one hour: a 20 m² mural usually needs 2–3 working days. We give you the exact timing in the quote.

How high can you print?

Maximum print height is 4 metres and width is unlimited. The wall must be at least 1.7 m high.
