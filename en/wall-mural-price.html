How much does a mural
printed on the wall cost?

Every wall is different, so we don't publish a single rate. The price depends on four factors, and we give it to you as a fixed quote, with a render of the mural on your wall, within 24 hours.

The price

What the price of a mural depends on

Square metres

The main factor. As a guide, the machine prints about 1 m² per hour and set-up takes around an hour.

The surface

Plaster, brick, wood, stone, concrete, ceramic, metal or glass. Before every project we run a test on the surface.

The design

If you already have the file, we adapt it to the wall. If not, we prepare it from your idea. You always see the render before we print.

The location

Free travel within 30 km of Barcelona. For the rest of Catalonia, Spain and Andorra, it is included in the quote.

What the quote includes

  • A render of the mural on a photo of your wall, within 24 hours
  • A fixed price before we start, with no surprises
  • Direct printing on the wall with the wallPen E2, up to 4 m high with no width limit
  • A 5-year written guarantee on indoor work

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a price without you visiting the wall?

Yes. With a photo and approximate measurements we prepare the render and the quote within 24 hours. Before printing, we run a test on the surface.

Is the render free?

Yes. The preview render is free and without obligation: you see how the mural will look on your wall before you decide.

How long does a print take?

As a guide, the machine prints about 1 m² per hour and set-up takes around an hour: a 20 m² mural usually needs 2–3 working days.

Is travel included?

It is free within 30 km of Barcelona. For the rest of Catalonia, Spain and Andorra, we include it in the quote.

Send us a photo of your wall

Tell us the measurements and what the space is for. Within 24 hours you'll have the render and the quote, with no obligation.
