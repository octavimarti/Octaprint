Why on the wall

Made for spaces where hygiene and calm matter

Hygiene

A continuous, washable surface with no edges or seams for dirt to collect in, and no extra layer of material on the wall.

No closures

The ink dries instantly and has little odour. One person does the work, zone by zone or out of hours.

Nothing to pull off

Easy to renew

Frequently asked questions

Can you print while patients are on the ward?

Yes. The ink dries instantly and has little odour, and one person does the work. We plan it zone by zone or out of hours so the centre keeps running.

Can the wall be cleaned?

Yes. The cured ink is scratch-resistant and washable, and the wall stays continuous, with no seams.

What ink do you use?

Vegan, solvent-free UV ink, ISO-certified and made in Europe, cured by LED light as it prints. If the centre needs it, we request the technical documentation from the manufacturer.

How long does it last?

We give a 5-year written guarantee on indoor work. When you want a change, you paint over it.

Tell us the measurements and which area it's for. Within 24 hours you'll have the render and the quote, with no obligation.
